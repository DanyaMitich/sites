08Frequently asked questions

Is it complicated? I have no technical background

No — that is exactly what the System is made for. Our main goal is to remove the barrier between you and the System: installation and everyday use are designed for someone who is not into systems and settings. The bot in Telegram guides you step by step: it tells you what to tap and checks that it worked.

Where will my documents and messages go?

To your server (VPS). The System only gets what you give it; after installation it is entirely yours, the access is yours, and you decide which updates to accept. The cloud AI model receives what it needs for the task — sometimes including the contents of a file; the files themselves are stored with you. A mode in which no text leaves your server (the model runs right on your server) is in the works — we will announce it in Telegram when it is ready.

“AI makes up numbers”

The System takes numbers from your files, accounts and websites, and the calculations are done by ordinary programs, not by AI. A second AI model double-checks important conclusions, and the decisions — and everything that goes out to people — stay with you.

“I’ll get a huge bill”

A strong AI model comes with a subscription, so its price is known in advance. Inexpensive models are pay-as-you-go. Set a spending limit in your account with each model: the model cannot charge beyond it. The System makes paid purchases and orders only with your consent.

What if foreign models get blocked?

The AI model in the System can be replaced: memory, files and settings are stored on your server. You can choose models paid in rubles.

Why a server and not my own computer?

A computer sleeps at night, while a server runs around the clock. At night the System gathers and calculates, and in the morning the result is ready. Your own computer stays free.

How soon will the System become useful?

From the first days it takes on the tasks you give it. In the first weeks the System learns your affairs and fills its databases, and every week it knows more: summaries get fuller and suggestions more accurate.

Do I need a foreign bank card?

No. The server and the DeepSeek, YandexGPT and GigaChat models are paid in rubles. A foreign card is only useful if you want to connect the best foreign models — Claude, ChatGPT and others. If you don’t have one, the [“Plati po vsemu miru”](https://platipomiru.com/?code=E2GQQ12Y) service (in Russian) can help: it pays for any foreign services, including model subscriptions, with a Russian card.

Where do I rent a server?

From any hosting provider — the server is rented in your name. We ourselves keep our servers at [Timeweb](https://timeweb.cloud/?i=148969) and [AdminVPS](https://my.adminvps.ru/aff.php?aff=32789). AdminVPS is cheaper, but its terms prohibit collecting data from websites (for example, monitoring listings and prices); if your System needs that, choose Timeweb. The installation bot will tell you how much power you need.

What if something breaks?

Updates arrive as a message in Telegram, and if something goes wrong, you can roll back to the previous version. The Telegram channel with updates is free for the first three months. Ongoing support is not included in the installation price.

How is the contract arranged?

Installation is under a public offer agreement: a contract that is accepted by making the payment. We will show it to you before payment. We go through the installation with you in Telegram, step by step.

Can I stop working with you?

Yes. The server and the data are yours. The System will keep running without us.
