## Frequently asked questions

Tap a question to read the answer.

Is it complicated? I have no technical knowledge

No — that's exactly what the System is made for. Our main goal is to remove the barrier between you and the System: installation and everyday use are designed for someone who doesn't know about systems and settings. A guide in Telegram takes you step by step: tells you what to press and checks the result.

Where will my photos, documents and correspondence go?

To your server (VPS). The System is separated from everything you have now and receives only what you give it. Only you have access to it: after installation you are its full owner and you decide which updates to accept. The cloud AI model receives what it needs for the task, while the files are stored with you. We recommend not putting personal data into the System until the installation is finished: once it's done, the window for the installation program closes.

Do my family members need to install anything?

No. The System runs only on your side. Your family opens the family page with a link and password.

"AI makes up numbers"

The System takes numbers from your files, accounts and websites, and the calculations are done by ordinary programs, not AI. Important conclusions are double-checked by a second AI model, and decisions — and everything that goes out to people — are yours.

"I'll get a huge bill"

The strong AI model is on a subscription, so its price is known in advance. Inexpensive models are pay-as-you-go, and the System shows the spending. Set a spending limit in each model's account: the model won't charge beyond it. Paid purchases, orders and emails to people happen only with your consent.

ChatGPT already has memory — why do I need the System?

ChatGPT's memory lives with the provider and works only inside ChatGPT: switch models and you start from scratch. If you change therapists every six months, you're getting to know therapists, not yourself. It's the same with AI. In the System your history — tasks, files, skills, goals — is stored on your server (VPS), and it works with whichever model you choose now and whichever you switch to later.

What if Claude or ChatGPT gets blocked?

The AI model in the System can be replaced: memory, files and settings are stored on your server, not in someone else's account. A model is like a bead: the beads change, but the thread with your history stays. You can choose models paid in roubles — Russian and Chinese models are getting better every month.

Why a server and not my computer?

A computer sleeps at night, but a server works around the clock. At night the System collects and calculates, and by morning the result is ready. Meanwhile your computer stays free.

How soon will the System become useful?

From day one it takes on the tasks you give it. In the first weeks the System learns your affairs and fills its databases, and every week it knows more: summaries get fuller and suggestions more precise.

Do I need a foreign card?

No. The server and the DeepSeek, YandexGPT and GigaChat models are paid in roubles. A foreign card is only needed if you want to connect the best foreign models — Claude, ChatGPT and others. If you don't have one, the service ["Plati po vsemu miru" (in Russian)](https://platipomiru.com/?code=E2GQQ12Y) can help: it pays for any foreign services with a Russian card, including model subscriptions.

Where do I rent a server (VPS)?

From any hosting provider — the server is rented in your name. We ourselves keep our servers at [Timeweb](https://timeweb.cloud/?i=148969) and [AdminVPS](https://my.adminvps.ru/aff.php?aff=32789). AdminVPS is cheaper, but its terms forbid collecting data from websites (for example, tracking listings and prices); if your System needs that, choose Timeweb. The installation bot will tell you how much capacity your tasks need.

Do I need a VPN?

No. We don't use VPNs and don't offer one. If you're in Russia and the best foreign AI models (such as Claude) or Gmail don't open for you, we recommend hynet — a personal foreign server that lets your phone and computer open foreign models and Gmail: [hynet.click (in Russian)](https://hynet.click/?r=194266790869). It's a separate service, not ours: plans from RUB 210 a month, you pay in its own account, and the installation bot will help you connect it. Check in a minute whether you need it: open claude.ai. If you see the login page, you don't need hynet. If it says the service isn't available in your country, or it or Gmail won't open, you do.

Updates arrive as a Telegram message, and if something goes wrong you can roll back to the previous version: all settings changes are logged. The Telegram updates channel is free for the first three months; ongoing support after installation is not included in the price.

Can I stop working with you?

Yes. The server and the data are yours: you are fully independent of us and of everyone else. But we'll stay close — sharing our developments and updates.

How it works

## Your System — on your server

The System is a set of AI agents — programs that carry out tasks on their own; among them is Hermes Agent, a well-known open-source AI agent. It runs on a virtual private server (VPS) — a computer on the internet rented in your name.

### Your server

Data, memory and settings live on your server, not in someone else's account. You are fully independent — of us and of everyone else. But we'll be close by, sharing our work and updates.

### Your model

You choose the AI model — the System's “brain”: Claude, Codex (from OpenAI) or models paid in roubles (DeepSeek, YandexGPT, GigaChat). All the integrations and capabilities of these systems are available to you, including Claude Science. The model can be swapped without losing memory or files.

### Your “yes”

The System gathers, calculates, drafts and reminds. Key actions — payment, sending, registration — the agent leaves to you.

### Everything is logged

What the System did and when is recorded in the work log. You can open it at any time.

### Spending stays within your limit

For pay-as-you-go models, you set the limit yourself in the provider's account — you will not be charged more. The System shows how much has been spent.

### Someone keeps an eye on the System

A separate watchdog program checks that everything works. If something breaks or hangs, you will get a message in Telegram.
