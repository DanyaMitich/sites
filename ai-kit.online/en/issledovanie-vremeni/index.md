That leaves about an hour and a half a day. This is where the tasks fall: reading important news, finding an answer, ordering groceries, sorting email, paying bills, choosing a holiday. ## How we calculated

The method in five steps
- We took the total time online according to Mediascope and separated out leisure.
- For each of the 17 activities we estimated the minutes per occasion and the frequency for three scenarios. Rare events, such as job hunting, are multiplied by the probability that they happen this year.
- For each option we estimated what share of time it takes off and multiplied it by the share of cases in which it is actually used. A chatbot and a paid assistant work only on request: they are used in 60–85 % of daily cases. A voice assistant is always at hand — 80–100 %. The [System's](https://ai-kit.online/) schedule fires on its own — 85–100 %.
- We subtracted the time spent on the tool itself: learning it, checking answers, confirmations. For the System in the first year this is 15–45 hours depending on the scenario, afterwards — 10–30.
- The range is the spread from a cautious to a bold estimate. We take its midpoint as the expected value.
- Work a person would not have time to do alone is counted separately — as the time the same coverage would take by hand. It is not included in the savings.
