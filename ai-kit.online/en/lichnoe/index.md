## What the System does in your personal affairs

01

### Shopping: the System finds, compares and catches the price

Before. Choosing a vacuum cleaner takes an evening in browser tabs: specs, reviews, prices in five stores. And a week later it gets cheaper, when you have already bought it.

Now. You write what you need and your budget. The System compares options by specifications and buyer reviews, filters out fake ratings and shows the two or three best with prices in different stores. If you are in no hurry, it tracks the price and writes when it drops.

02

### Coach: support on the way to your goals

Before. A language, sport, a book, a new profession: starting is easy, but two weeks later everything goes back to the way it was. There is no one to remind you, support you and honestly show you where you stand.

Now. You name your goals — books, sport, languages, study — and the System builds a plan for each and guides you every day: short tasks, reminders at a convenient time, a check of what has stuck. Progress is measured honestly: for a language, how many words you remembered a week later, not how many minutes you spent in the app. You choose the tone: gentle, businesslike or strict — like a coach who gives no slack.

03

### Bills and payments: all the month's bills in one list

Before. Kindergarten, clubs, classes, the apartment: each has its own bill and its own person who sends it. At the end of the month you have to scroll through chats and remember who is owed how much.

Now. You forward bills and receipts to the System — to Telegram or by email. The System keeps a list: to whom, for what, how much and by what date. On the right day it sends it in full, with a payment link or the QR code from the bill, if there is one. You pay yourself: the System does not send money.

04

### Employment: vacancies selected, applications sent every day

Before. Job hunting means hundreds of vacancies, identical applications and silence in response. There is no time to write a cover letter for each vacancy, so few applications go out.

Now. Every day the System reviews new vacancies matching your experience and wishes, filters out the unsuitable ones and writes a cover letter for each. You approve — the application goes out. Employers' replies are collected in one list, and before an interview the System prepares a briefing on the company.

05

### Complex search: archives, registries, old documents

Before. Sometimes the answer is not in a search engine but in an archive, a registry or a scan half a century old. Searching by hand takes months: dozens of sites, each with its own login and rules, and handwritten text cannot be read.

Now. The System searches dozens of archives, registries and databases, including those that require logging in with your account. It reads scans and handwriting, cross-checks sources against each other and keeps a log: every find comes with a snapshot of the source, and dead ends are recorded too. If the answer exists only at an institution, the System prepares a request letter. Finds reach you in Telegram right away.

06

### News and videos — in a short digest, as text or voice

Before. Channels, newsletters, hour-long videos and podcasts: staying informed takes two hours a day. Half of it is repeats, and the important things get lost.

Now. You give a list of channels, sites and video blogs. Every morning the System condenses them into one short digest: what is new and why it matters to you. A long video becomes a summary of five minutes' reading. The digest can be listened to by voice on the road — as an episode in your podcast feed.

07

### Events for your hobby — in your calendar, with a reminder

Before. You learn about an interesting workshop, tournament or concert when it is already over. Listings are scattered across dozens of sites and channels.

Now. You name your hobbies, your city and what interests you — concerts, cinema, lectures and courses, festivals, forums. Every day the System browses listings, clubs and channels, picks what suits you and puts it in your calendar with a reminder. Without your “yes” the System does not register you or buy tickets.

08

### A private family page: news, photos and family matters in one place

Before. Photos are in five chats, birthdays are in your head, shared plans get lost in the correspondence. Relatives in other cities hear the news last.

Now. The System keeps a private page for your family: password access, for your own people only. Photos and news from the family chat gather there by themselves, along with a shared calendar — birthdays, trips, doctor visits — and shopping and to-do lists. Older relatives can read the page in large print or listen to it by voice. Relatives need to install nothing — a link and a password are enough.

09

### Important documents sorted, deadlines watched

Before. Passports, policies, contracts, warranties and receipts are scattered across different folders and photos on your phone. You remember an insurance or passport expiry date when it has already passed.

Now. You photograph a document or forward an email. The System recognizes the text, files the document in the right folder on your VPS and pulls out the essentials: number, dates, amounts, terms. Before each deadline there is a reminder: renew the policy, replace the passport, return the goods under warranty. The document you need is found with one phrase.

10

### Legal review for your personal questions

Before. A lease, buying an apartment, a dispute with a store or a management company. Reading the law yourself takes long, and a lawyer's consultation costs money even when the question is simple.

Now. You send a contract or describe the situation. The System finds risky clauses and the relevant articles of law, checks them against court practice and verifies its own conclusion with a second AI model. The output is a plain review: what is fine, where the risk is, what to ask and how to reply. Where a lawyer is needed, the System says so and prepares questions for them.

## What people usually worry about — and what we have done about it

01

### "AI makes up numbers"

The System takes numbers from your files, accounts and websites, and ordinary programs, not AI, do the calculations. A second AI model double-checks every important conclusion. AI is a developing technology and progress is accelerating: you need to learn to work with it, like any other skill. For that we have the project [be-profi.online](https://be-profi.online/).

02

### “Work data must not be handed over to foreign neural networks”

You choose the model: Claude, Russian services paid for in rubles, or — on a powerful VPS — models running right on your own server, in which case the text goes nowhere. A cloud model receives only the text of the task it is called in for; the files themselves are stored on your VPS.

03

### What if Claude or ChatGPT gets blocked?

That does happen in current conditions. But other ways of setting up and installing are appearing, and models from Russia and China improve in quality every month and, by our estimate, will soon catch up with the best ones. The AI model in the System can be replaced: memory, files and settings are stored on your VPS, not in someone else's account.

04

### "I'll get a huge bill"

A strong AI model comes on a fixed-price subscription, inexpensive ones are billed by the amount of work, and the System shows you the spending. Be sure to set a spending limit in each model's personal account: the model will not charge anything beyond the limit. For heavy tasks, the System rents a computer with a graphics card only for the duration of the task and only after your “yes” to a quoted approximate price. Then it returns it to the provider and reports how long it ran and the approximate amount.

05

### “The AI will do something without asking”

The System prepares emails, messages and posts as drafts — you send them with a single “yes”. It will also pay or register on websites only after your “yes”.

06

### “It will break and nobody will notice”

A separate watchdog program checks that everything is working: if something breaks or hangs, you get a message in Telegram. And what the System did and when is recorded in the work log.

07

### “Will it be perfect right away?”

Honestly: no. In the first weeks the System learns your affairs, the databases are still empty, and the reports are shorter. The longer it works, the more it knows and the more useful its summaries become.

Questions

## Frequently asked questions

Does the legal review replace a lawyer?

No. The System reads the document, finds risky clauses and the relevant articles of law, checks its conclusion with a second AI model and prepares questions for a lawyer. The decision and the signature are yours, and in disputed cases you make them together with a lawyer.

Who will see the family page?

Only those you have given access to. The page is password-protected and sits on your VPS; after the System is handed over, we have no access to it.

How is the System different from ChatGPT?

ChatGPT only knows what you pasted into the conversation. The System itself pulls data from your accounts and services, works on a schedule without you asking, and keeps all past calculations on your VPS.

I'm not an IT person. Who will look after the System?

The System looks after itself. Every night it copies its data. A watchdog program checks that everything works and messages you if something has stopped. Improvements to the System come through the updates channel, 3 months free. One-off customisations are by separate agreement. You use the System through Telegram and your personal website.

How much does it cost per month?

Your costs are VPS rental and payment for AI models. You pay them directly to the providers. How much that comes to is in the "Your costs per month" block above.

Do I need a subscription to foreign models?

For the basics — no. If you already have a subscription to the best foreign models (Claude, ChatGPT and others), the bot will connect it in a few steps. If you have no foreign card, you can pay for the subscription with a Russian card via the ["Pay Worldwide"](https://platipomiru.com/?code=E2GQQ12Y) service.

Where do I rent a VPS?

From any hosting provider — the VPS is rented in your name. We ourselves keep servers at [Timeweb](https://timeweb.cloud/?i=148969) and [AdminVPS](https://my.adminvps.ru/aff.php?aff=32789). AdminVPS is cheaper, but its terms forbid collecting data from websites (for example, tracking listings and prices); if the System needs that, choose Timeweb. The installation bot will tell you how much power you need.

What if the VPS is not powerful enough?

VPS power can be increased in your hosting control panel. And for heavy tasks — transcribing many recordings, running your own AI model, generating images or 3D — the System has short-term rental. The System takes a computer with a graphics card from a Russian provider only for the duration of the task and pays for it from your account with that provider. It names the approximate time and price in advance and starts only after your "yes". When the work is done, the result stays on your VPS and the computer goes back to the provider: payment stops immediately and copies of your files on it are erased. At the end the System tells you how many hours it ran and the approximate cost; the exact amount is in your account with the provider. For transcribing recordings the System has a ready-made program; for other tasks the System prepares a program and agrees it with you. You need to open an account with the provider and top it up once — about 10 minutes.

Why a VPS and not my computer?

Your computer sleeps at night, while a VPS works around the clock. At night the System collects and calculates, and in the morning the result is already ready. Your computer stays free, and you can switch it off whenever you like.

Can I stop working with you?

Yes. The VPS and the data are yours. The System will keep working without us.
