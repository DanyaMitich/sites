## What the System takes over

01

### For an independent real estate agent: a voice note after a viewing becomes a client note, a draft message and a reminder

Before. The viewing is over, two more to go. What the client disliked, what you agreed on, whom to call back — you keep it in your head or in phone notes. By evening half of it is forgotten, and a client who did not hear from you in time goes to another agent.

Now. On the way back from the viewing you talk for a couple of minutes. The System transcribes the recording, notes what matters to the client and what was agreed, drafts a message and sets a reminder. You send the message yourself: copy the text or edit it together with the System. Recordings and notes are stored on your VPS; the text goes for analysis to the AI model you have chosen. Getting the client’s consent to record is up to you.

02

### For an independent consultant: a meeting turns into a summary, an email and a reminder

Before. After a session with a client you need to write down what was agreed, send a summary and remember to check whether the client did what they promised. That takes an hour you don’t have — so the summary goes out three days later, or never.

Now. You send the meeting recording. The System makes a transcript and a short summary: what was decided, who does what and by when. It drafts an email to the client and reminds you of deadlines. Recordings and notes are stored on your VPS; the text goes for analysis to the AI model you have chosen. Getting the client’s consent to record is up to you.

03

### For an independent lawyer: case timeline and deadlines from court rulings

Before. To prepare an appeal, you have to read the first-instance judgment and the appellate ruling, write down what each court established and calculate the procedural deadlines. That is an evening of work after office hours.

Now. You send the court rulings. The System writes out the timeline, what each court established and where they differ, and calculates deadlines with dates. Every point has a page reference and a quote so you can check it yourself. Conclusions and the legal position are yours. While the System works with external AI models, we advise giving it public rulings and anonymised materials rather than documents covered by attorney–client privilege: court rulings, published case law, your templates without names.

## What people usually worry about — and what has been done about it

“AI makes up numbers”

The System takes numbers from your files, accounts and websites, and the calculations are done by ordinary programs, not by AI. Important conclusions are double-checked by a second AI model. AI is an evolving technology and progress is speeding up: working with it is a skill you learn, like any other. That is what our project [be-profi.online](https://be-profi.online/) (in Russian) is for.

“Work data must not be given to foreign AI”

You choose the model: Claude, Russian services paid in rubles or — on a powerful VPS — models running right on your server, so the text goes nowhere. A cloud model receives the text of the task it is called for; the files themselves stay on your VPS.

What if Claude or ChatGPT gets blocked?

In today’s conditions this does happen. But other ways to configure and install keep appearing, and models from Russia and China improve every month and, in our view, will soon catch up with the best. The AI model in the System can be replaced: memory, files and settings are stored on your VPS, not in someone else’s account.

“I’ll get a huge bill”

A strong AI model is a subscription with a fixed price; inexpensive ones are paid by usage, and the System shows what you spend. Be sure to set a spending limit in the account of each model: the model cannot charge beyond the limit. For heavy tasks the System rents a GPU computer only for the duration of the task and only after you say “yes” to the stated approximate price. Then it returns the computer to the provider and tells you how long it worked and the approximate cost.

“The AI will do something without asking”

The System prepares emails, messages and posts as drafts — you send them with a single “yes”. It will also pay and sign up on websites only after your “yes”.

“It will break and nobody will notice”

A separate watchdog program checks that everything works: if something breaks or hangs, you will get a message in Telegram. And what the System did and when is recorded in the work log.

“Will it be perfect right away?”

Honestly, no. In the first weeks the System is learning your business, the databases are still empty and reports are shorter. The longer it works, the more it knows and the more useful its summaries become.

## Frequently asked questions

Do I need a subscription to foreign models?

Not for Basic. If you already have a subscription to the best foreign models (Claude, ChatGPT and others), the bot connects it in a few steps. If you don’t have a foreign card, the subscription can be paid with a Russian card through the [“Plati po vsemu miru”](https://platipomiru.com/?code=E2GQQ12Y) service (in Russian).

Where do I rent a VPS?

From any hosting provider — the VPS is rented in your name. We ourselves keep servers at [Timeweb](https://timeweb.cloud/?i=148969) and [AdminVPS](https://my.adminvps.ru/aff.php?aff=32789). AdminVPS is cheaper, but its terms forbid collecting data from websites (for example, tracking listings and prices); if your System needs that, choose Timeweb. The setup bot will tell you how much capacity you need.

What if the VPS is not powerful enough?

VPS capacity can be increased in the hosting provider’s account. And for heavy tasks — transcribing many recordings, running your own AI model, generating images or 3D — the System has short-term rental. It rents a GPU computer from a Russian provider only for the duration of the task and pays from your account with that provider. It states the approximate time and price in advance and starts only after your “yes”. When the work is done, the result stays on your VPS and the computer goes back to the provider: billing stops immediately and copies of your files on it are erased. At the end the System tells you how many hours it worked and the approximate cost; the exact amount is in your account with the provider. For transcribing recordings the System has a ready-made program; for other tasks it prepares a program and agrees it with you. Once, you need to open an account with the provider and top it up — about 10 minutes.

Why a VPS and not my own computer?

A computer sleeps at night, while a VPS runs around the clock. At night the System collects and calculates, and by morning the result is ready. Your computer stays free, and you can switch it off whenever you like.

Can I stop working with you?

Yes. The VPS and the data are yours. The System will keep working without us.
